## [THE PROBLEM]

### Communities Struggle Without Feedback

| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Members can't find quality groups | Join randomly, leave disappointed |
| Group admins lack feedback | Can't improve without insights |
| No quality indicators | Good and bad groups look the same |
| New members churn early | Couldn't find their people |

---

## [THE SOLUTION]

### Transparent, Member-Driven Reviews

Let your community members rate and review groups with:

- **Multi-criteria star ratings** you customize
- **Review moderation** for quality control
- **Seamless BuddyPress integration**
- **Prominent directory display**
