## Tips & Education Posts

**Post 1:**
Tip for BuddyPress communities:

Don't expect reviews to magically appear.

Ask your most engaged members to leave the first reviews.
Make it easy (clear button, simple form).
Show reviews prominently.

First reviews create momentum.

---

**Post 2:**
Group review best practice:

Keep auto-approval OFF.

Let group admins curate reviews.

Result: Higher quality feedback, no spam, more trust.
