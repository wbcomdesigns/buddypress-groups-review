## Educational Posts

### Post 1: Tips

**5 ways to get more group reviews in your BuddyPress community:**

1. **Ask directly** - Reach out to active members and ask them to review their favorite group

2. **Make it visible** - Add the review widget to your sidebar so ratings are always in sight

3. **Lead by example** - Have moderators review groups they're NOT admin of

4. **Enable activity posts** - When reviews show up in the feed, others are reminded to review too

5. **Celebrate reviews** - Give a shoutout when a group gets a great review

First reviews create momentum. After that, it becomes natural.

Using BuddyPress Group Reviews? What's working for you?

### Post 2: Best Practices

**Should you auto-approve group reviews? Here's what I recommend:**

Short answer: No.

Longer answer:

Auto-approval means every review goes live immediately. Sounds efficient, but...

- Spam reviews get published
- Inappropriate content slips through
- Group admins have no control

With moderation ON:
- Group admins see new reviews first
- One-click approve or deny
- Quality stays high
- Members trust the reviews more

The extra step is worth it.

What's your preference? Auto-approve or moderated?

### Post 3: How-To

**How to set up group reviews in 5 minutes:**

If you're running BuddyPress or BuddyBoss, here's the quick setup:

**Step 1:** Install "BuddyPress Group Reviews" from WordPress plugins

**Step 2:** Go to Settings and add your rating criteria (Communication, Helpfulness, Activity, etc.)

**Step 3:** Keep auto-approval OFF so group admins can moderate

**Step 4:** Enable notifications so admins know when reviews arrive

**Step 5:** Add the rating widget to your sidebar

Done! Members can now review groups from the Reviews tab.

## Facebook-Specific Tips

### Formatting
- Shorter paragraphs (1-2 sentences)
- Line breaks between sections
- Questions encourage comments
- Images/screenshots increase engagement

### Tone
- More casual than LinkedIn
- Community-focused language
- Conversational, friendly

### Best Practices
- Respond to all comments
- Ask questions to drive engagement
- Use native video when possible
- Avoid external links in initial posts (add in first comment)

### Posting Frequency
- 3-5 posts per week maximum
- Vary content types
- Best times: 9am, 1pm, 3pm

### Groups vs Page
- Share educational content in WordPress/BuddyPress groups
- Promotional content on company page
- Engage authentically in group discussions
