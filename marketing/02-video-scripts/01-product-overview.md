# Product Overview Video Script

**Title:** BuddyPress Group Reviews - Give Your Community a Voice
**Duration:** 2:30 - 3:00 minutes
**Purpose:** Introduce the plugin and its core value proposition
**Tone:** Professional, friendly, solution-focused
**Target Audience:** Community managers, WordPress site owners

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:15)

**VISUAL:** BuddyPress group directory with rating stars visible

**NARRATOR:**
"Running a BuddyPress community? You probably have dozens of groups—but do you know which ones your members actually love?"

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Which groups are worth joining?"

---

### THE PROBLEM (0:15 - 0:35)

**VISUAL:** Member scrolling through groups, looking confused

**NARRATOR:**

**VISUAL:** Empty group page with tumbleweeds (animated)

**NARRATOR:**
"Meanwhile, group admins get zero feedback on what's working—or what needs to change."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "No feedback = No improvement"

**NARRATOR:**

**NARRATOR:**
"Members share their experiences. Group admins get actionable feedback. And newcomers find great groups instantly."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Transparency builds trust"

## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- Clean, professional look matching BuddyPress aesthetic
- Consistent color scheme (blues, whites)
- Smooth transitions between scenes
- Real plugin screenshots/screencasts

### Music
- Upbeat, modern, professional
- Not too energetic—this is B2B software
- Fade under narration, up during transitions

### Screenshots Needed
1. Group directory with ratings visible
2. Single group page with Reviews tab
3. Review submission form
4. Moderation queue with pending reviews
5. Settings pages (criteria, display, emails)
6. BuddyPress notification
7. Activity stream with review
8. Widget in sidebar

### Voiceover Notes
- Professional, friendly tone
- Clear articulation
- Moderate pace—not rushed
- Emphasis on benefit statements
