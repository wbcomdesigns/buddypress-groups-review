## Production Notes for All Ads

### Visual Style
- Fast cuts for short ads
- Clean, professional aesthetic
- Plugin screenshots where relevant
- Animated text overlays

### Music
- Upbeat but not distracting
- Licensed for commercial use
- Fade under voiceover

### Call-to-Action
- Always end with clear CTA
- "Download Free" or "Learn More"
- Include WordPress.org or wbcomdesigns.com

### Platform Specifications

| Platform | Format | Safe Zone | Max Duration |
|----------|--------|-----------|--------------|
| YouTube Pre-roll | 16:9 | Center 80% | 15s/30s |
| Instagram Stories | 9:16 | Avoid top/bottom 15% | 15s |
| Facebook Feed | 1:1 or 4:5 | Center | 60s |
| LinkedIn | 16:9 or 1:1 | Center | 30s |
| TikTok | 9:16 | Avoid top 20% | 15s-60s |
