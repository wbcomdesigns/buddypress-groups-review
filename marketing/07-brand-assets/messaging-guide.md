# Messaging Guide - BuddyPress Group Reviews

---

## Brand Voice

### Tone Attributes
- **Confident** but not arrogant - We know our product solves real problems
- **Helpful** and solution-focused - Always lead with the benefit
- **Professional** yet friendly - Approachable without being casual
- **Direct** without jargon - Clear language anyone can understand
- **Empowering** - Focus on what users can achieve

### Voice Guidelines
- Use "you" more than "we"
- Focus on outcomes, not outputs
- Lead with benefits, follow with features
- Use active voice
- Keep sentences concise

**"Give your community a voice with group reviews that build trust and drive engagement."**

BuddyPress Group Reviews lets your community members rate and review groups, so everyone can find the best groups to join. With multi-criteria ratings, review moderation, and seamless BuddyPress integration, you'll build a more engaged, trustworthy community where quality rises to the top.

Transform your BuddyPress community with member-driven group reviews. Let members share their experiences, help newcomers discover high-quality groups, and give group admins the feedback they need to improve. With customizable rating criteria, powerful moderation tools, activity stream integration, and Google-ready SEO markup, you'll create a transparent community where the best groups get the recognition they deserve.

## Feature-to-Benefit Messaging

### Multi-Criteria Rating System

**Feature:** Customizable rating criteria with star ratings

**Benefit Message:**
Understand exactly what members love and what needs work—so you can focus improvements where they matter most.

**Why It Matters:**
- Custom criteria let you measure what matters to YOUR community
- Multiple rating factors provide nuanced feedback
- Star ratings are instantly recognizable and trustworthy

---

### Review Moderation

**Feature:** Approve/deny reviews before publishing

**Benefit Message:**
Maintain quality standards without heavy-handed censorship—publish authentic reviews while filtering spam.

**Why It Matters:**
- Group admins control what gets published
- Prevents spam and inappropriate content
- Builds trust with authentic, curated reviews

---

### BuddyPress Notifications

**Feature:** Native BuddyPress notification integration

**Benefit Message:**
Never miss important feedback—get instant alerts when members share their thoughts about your group.

**Why It Matters:**
- Group admins stay informed in real-time
- Members know their feedback is being seen
- Faster response to issues and compliments

---

### Activity Stream Integration

**Feature:** Reviews posted to BuddyPress activity feed

**Benefit Message:**
Turn every review into community content that sparks conversations and showcases active groups.

**Why It Matters:**
- Increases review visibility
- Drives more engagement with groups
- Creates organic social proof

---

### Email Notifications

**Feature:** Customizable email alerts for submissions, approvals, and denials

**Benefit Message:**
Keep everyone in the loop with automated notifications—even when they're not logged in.

**Why It Matters:**
- Group admins get notified of new reviews instantly
- Members know when their review is published
- Customizable messages match your brand voice

---

**Why It Matters:**

## Phrases to Avoid

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "We offer..." | "You get..." |
| "Our plugin does..." | "With Group Reviews, you can..." |
| "Users can..." | "You can..." / "Your members can..." |
| "It's easy" | "Takes just 5 minutes" (be specific) |
| "Best in class" | Specific proof points |
| "Revolutionary" | Specific benefits |
| "State-of-the-art" | Actual technology benefits |
