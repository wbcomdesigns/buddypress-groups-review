## Slide 2: The Problem

### Communities Struggle Without Feedback

**Members can't find quality groups**
- They join random groups and leave disappointed
- No way to see which groups are active and valuable

**Group admins lack insight**
- Don't know what members actually think
- Can't improve without feedback

**Community managers fly blind**
- No visibility into group quality
- Can't identify what's working vs. what isn't

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

### Transparent, Member-Driven Reviews

**BuddyPress Group Reviews** lets members rate and review groups with:

- Multi-criteria star ratings
- Moderated text reviews
- Seamless BuddyPress integration

**Result:** Trust, transparency, and better groups.

---

## Slide 4: How It Works

### Simple 4-Step Process

**1. Members Visit Groups**
Browse the group directory with ratings visible

**2. Members Submit Reviews**
Rate criteria + write feedback from the Reviews tab

**3. Admins Moderate**
Approve or deny reviews before publishing

**4. Reviews Go Live**
Published reviews appear on groups and in activity feeds
