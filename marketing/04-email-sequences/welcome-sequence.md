## Email 2: Key Settings to Configure

**Trigger:** Email 1 sent
**Delay:** 2 days
**Subject Line Options:**
- A: 3 settings to configure right now
- B: Don't skip these settings (quick 5-minute setup)

---

**Body:**

Hi,

Let's make sure your BuddyPress Group Reviews setup is optimized. Here are the 3 settings that make the biggest difference:

---

**1. Rating Criteria (The Most Important One)**

Default criteria are fine, but custom criteria get better feedback.

Think about what matters in YOUR community:
- "Responsiveness" for support groups
- "Fun factor" for hobby groups
- "Networking value" for professional groups

**Set up criteria:** Settings → Criteria tab

---

**2. Review Moderation**

I recommend keeping auto-approval OFF. This:
- Prevents spam and abuse
- Gives group admins control
- Maintains review quality

Group admins can approve reviews with one click from their Manage tab.

---

**3. Email Notifications**

Don't let reviews go unnoticed! Enable email notifications so:
- Group admins get notified of new submissions
- Reviewers know when their review is approved

Customize the email templates to match your brand voice.

**Configure emails:** Settings → Emails tab

---

That's it for the essentials. Tomorrow I'll show you how to promote reviews to your members.

Questions? Just reply to this email.

Cheers,
The Wbcom Designs Team

## Email 3: Getting Your First Reviews

**Trigger:** Email 2 sent
**Delay:** 2 days
**Subject Line Options:**
- A: How to get your first reviews (and make them count)
- B: 4 ways to encourage group reviews

---

**Body:**

Hi,

Your plugin is configured. Now let's get some reviews flowing!

**Here are 4 proven ways to encourage group reviews:**

---

**1. Ask Group Admins to Invite Reviews**

Group admins have the most influence. Suggest they:
- Post in their group asking for feedback
- Personally ask active members to review
- Mention reviews during events or meetups

**Template they can use:**
*"Help future members find us! If you've enjoyed this group, take 2 minutes to leave a review in our Reviews tab. Your feedback helps others discover what we're all about."*

---

**2. Feature Reviews in Activity**

Enable activity stream integration (Settings → General). When reviews post to the feed, other members see them and are reminded they can review too.

---

**3. Showcase Reviews with Widgets**

Add the Group Rating Widget to your sidebar. When members see star ratings prominently displayed, they're more likely to contribute their own.

---

**4. Lead by Example**

Have your community manager or moderators review a few groups (ones they're not admin of, of course). First reviews break the ice.

---

**Pro tip:** Don't expect floods of reviews immediately. Even 2-3 reviews per group dramatically improves trust and discovery.

Tomorrow: How to handle negative reviews constructively.

Cheers,
The Wbcom Designs Team

## Email 4: Handling Negative Reviews

**Trigger:** Email 3 sent
**Delay:** 2 days
**Subject Line Options:**
- A: What to do when you get a negative review
- B: Negative reviews aren't the enemy (here's why)

---

**Body:**

Hi,

At some point, a group will get a less-than-glowing review. That's actually a good thing. Here's why:

---

**Negative reviews = Authenticity**

All 5-star reviews look suspicious. A mix of ratings tells members the reviews are real.

---

**What to do with negative reviews:**

**1. Read it objectively**
Is there valid feedback? Many "negative" reviews contain actionable insights.

**2. Decide on moderation**
Approve if it's:
- Honest feedback (even if unflattering)
- Specific about issues
- Not abusive or spammy

Deny if it's:
- Personal attacks
- Spam or off-topic
- Violates community guidelines

**3. Take action if needed**
If a group gets consistent criticism, reach out to the group admin. Help them improve. That's the whole point!

**4. Consider responding**
Group admins can't reply to reviews directly, but they can:
- Address concerns in a group post
- Make changes and announce them
- Thank members for feedback

---

**Remember:** The goal isn't perfect ratings. It's better groups.

A group that improves based on feedback becomes a better experience for everyone—and that member who left the critical review might become your biggest advocate.

One more email tomorrow with some power-user tips!

Cheers,
The Wbcom Designs Team

## Email 5: Power User Tips

**Trigger:** Email 4 sent

- B: Advanced features you should know about

---

**Body:**

Hi,

You've been using BuddyPress Group Reviews for about a week now. Here are some power-user features you might not have discovered:

---

**1. Exclude Groups from Reviews**

Have admin-only groups or test groups? Exclude them from reviews so they don't clutter the experience.

Settings → General → Exclude Groups

---

**2. Custom Star Colors**

Match the star ratings to your brand. Gold is nice, but your brand color might be better.

Settings → Display → Rating Color

---

**3. Use Shortcodes for Custom Pages**

Want a dedicated reviews page or embed the form somewhere custom?

`[bgr_review_form group_id="123"]`

---

**4. Check the REST API (Developers)**

Building a mobile app or headless frontend? Reviews are accessible via the WordPress REST API.

---

**5. GamiPress Integration**

Using GamiPress? Reviews trigger events you can use to award points or badges. Great for encouraging more reviews!
