## Writing Tips for Feature Announcements

### Subject Lines
- Include [New Feature] or [Update] prefix for scanability
- Be specific about what's new
- Create curiosity or highlight benefit

### Body Structure
1. Lead with the biggest/most exciting change
2. Bullet secondary changes
3. Clear update instructions
4. Support contact

### Tone
- Excited but not hyperbolic
- Clear and concise
- Focus on user benefit
- Include action step

### Timing
- Major updates: Send within 24 hours of release
- Minor updates: Weekly digest or skip email
- Security updates: Immediate
