## Content Guidelines

### Tone
- Confident but not arrogant
- Helpful and solution-focused
- Professional yet friendly
- Direct without jargon

### Key Phrases
- "so you can..."
- "which means..."
- "without having to..."

### Avoid
- "We offer..." → Use "You get..."
- Vague superlatives → Use specific benefits
- Feature-first language → Lead with benefits
