# Quick Start

This walks a fresh install to a working review flow in a few minutes.

## 1. Confirm the criteria

Go to **WB Plugins, Group Reviews, Criteria**. The plugin ships with three criteria: Quality, Relevance, and Engagement. Add, remove, or disable criteria so members rate on what matters to your community. A review can still be submitted when no rating criteria are active (it becomes a text-only review).

## 2. Choose your moderation model

Go to the **General** tab.

- Leave **Auto approve reviews** off to hold every review as a draft until a group admin approves it (default).
- Turn it on to publish reviews immediately.

Set **Reviews per page** for the Manage Reviews list, and decide whether to allow BuddyPress notifications and activity entries.

## 3. Visit a group

Open any single group (that is not excluded). A member who belongs to the group, and is not its admin, sees two tabs:

- **Reviews** - the published reviews and the group's average rating.
- **Add Review** - the submission form.

Group admins additionally see a **Manage Reviews** tab for approving or denying pending reviews.

## 4. Submit and approve

As a member, open **Add Review**, rate each criterion, add an optional description, and submit. As the group admin, open **Manage Reviews**, then **Accept** or **Deny** the review. Once accepted, it appears under **Reviews** and counts toward the group's average.

## Next steps

- Change the tab and button labels and the star colour on the **Display** tab.
- Turn on emails on the **Emails** tab.
- Drop the review form or a group leaderboard on any page with [Shortcodes](../shortcodes/shortcodes.md).
