# Managing Reviews (Group Admin Guide)

**Included in Free**

1. Open your group and click **Manage**.
2. Open **Manage Review** (the name follows your Review Label) to see pending reviews.
3. Click **Approve** to publish or **Deny** to move it to the trash.
4. Use **Review Criteria** to change which criteria members rate.

You can manage reviews only for groups you administer. You cannot review your own group.
