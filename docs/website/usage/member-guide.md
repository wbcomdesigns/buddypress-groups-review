# Writing a Review (Member Guide)

**Included in Free**

## Write a review

1. Join the group. Only members can review.
2. Open the group and click the **Reviews** tab, then **Add Review**.
3. Give a title, write your review and click the stars for each criterion.
4. Submit.

![Review form](../images/add-review-form.webp)

If the site approves reviews first, you see a notice and your review appears once a group admin approves it.

## Who cannot review

- Group admins cannot review their own group.
- If multiple reviews are off, you can review a group once.
- Some groups are excluded by the site owner.

## Sort and browse

Use **Sort by** on the Reviews tab: newest, oldest, highest rated or lowest rated.

## On a phone

The list and the form stack to a single column.

![Reviews on a phone](../images/group-reviews-tab-mobile.webp)
