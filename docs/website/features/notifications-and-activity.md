# Notifications and Activity

**Included in Free**

Turn **BuddyPress Notifications** and **Review Activity** on or off on the General tab.

## Notifications

- Group admins are told when a review is submitted.
- The reviewer is told when a review is approved or denied.

## Emails

The **Emails** tab has three templates: submission, acceptance and denial. Each has its own on/off switch, subject and message. See [Control Review Emails](../how-to/control-review-emails.md).

## Activity

When **Review Activity** is on, an approved review posts to the group activity stream with its star rating.
