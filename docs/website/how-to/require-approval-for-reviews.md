# Require Approval Before Reviews Go Live

**Included in Free**

1. Open **WB Plugins > Group Reviews > General**.
2. Leave **Auto Approval of Reviews** off to hold every review for a group admin.
3. Switch it on to publish reviews as soon as they are submitted.
4. Click **Save Settings**.

Pending reviews show under **Manage > Manage Review** (the name follows your Review Label) on the group and under **Reviews** in wp-admin.
