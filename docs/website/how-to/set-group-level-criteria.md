# Let Group Admins Choose Their Own Criteria

**Included in Free**

1. Turn on **Group-Level Criteria** on the General tab.
2. A group admin opens the group, clicks **Manage**, then **Review Criteria**.
3. They choose **Use site-wide criteria** or **Customize criteria for this group**.
4. They enable the site-wide criteria they want, add custom ones and save.

Groups that do not customize keep following the site-wide list.
