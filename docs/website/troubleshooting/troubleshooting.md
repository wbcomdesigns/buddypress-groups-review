# Troubleshooting

## I cannot see the Reviews tab

- Confirm the BuddyPress **User Groups** component is on.
- Check the group is not in **Exclude Groups**.
- Private and hidden groups show reviews to members only.

## I cannot see Add Review

Group admins cannot review their own group. Non-members must join first. If multiple reviews are off, you can review a group once.

## My review does not appear

New reviews wait for approval unless **Auto Approval of Reviews** is on. A group admin can approve it under **Manage > Manage Review** (the name follows your Review Label).

## Only some reviews show on a page

The Reviews tab shows **Reviews Per Page** at a time. Use the page numbers under the list. The setting accepts 1 to 100.

## The star color does not change

Save the **Display** tab, then clear any page cache.

## Emails are not sent

Turn on **BuddyPress Notifications** (General) and the matching **Enable Email** switch (Emails). Check that your site can send mail.
